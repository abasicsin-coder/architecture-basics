# Architecture Basics — Fusion Architecture Website

A bespoke architectural website with **video scrollytelling**, **collapsible project portfolios**, **dual-track appointment bookings**, and a **live Admin CMS portal**.

---

## Quick Start (How to Launch)

You **do not need to write any commands or open any tools**:
1. Simply **double-click `Start-Website.bat`** in this folder.
2. It automatically starts a local server and opens the website in your browser at `http://localhost:8000`.
3. Keep the launcher window open while browsing. When you are done, simply close the black launcher window.

---

## Architectural Ethos & Aesthetics
- **Minimalistic quiet luxury palette**: Alabaster stone, raw travertine, muted charcoal, and subtle bronze accents. No loud or bold colors.
- **Fusion Architecture**: Harmonizes traditional vernacular craft (rammed earth, terracotta jali, stone ashlar) with contemporary minimalism.
- **Strict Zero-Waste Policy**: Material circularity, passive bioclimatic cooling, and zero landfill waste.
- **Zero Templates**: 100% bespoke design process tailored to site solar and geological contours.

## Key Features

### 1. Scrollytelling (Scroll-Driven Video Timeline)
- Powered by `Sequence 01.mp4` (encoded with keyframe distance = 1 for instant frame scrubbing).
- Scrolling the page smoothly scrubs the video frame by frame with choreographed architectural narrative cards fading in and out at key timestamps.
- Includes a live timeline scrub indicator (`0.00s`) and progress bar.

### 2. Collapsible Project Tiles
- 5 Architectural Typologies:
  1. **Commercial & Civic Projects**
  2. **Resorts & Eco-Sanctuaries**
  3. **Bespoke Private Villas**
  4. **Fusion Bungalows**
  5. **Sustainable Farmhouses**
- **Hover & Click interaction**: Hovering or clicking on any tile smoothly expands it to display the curated projects, LEED/environmental specs, and photo gallery.
- Clicking on any project card opens a full architectural detail modal with high-res photography and specifications.

### 3. Our Philosophy & The 4-Stage Process
- In-depth presentation of the studio's 4 core pillars.
- 4-Stage Process breakdown: Geological & Sun Dialogue → Vernacular Material Synthesis → Fusion Detailing → Zero-Waste Execution.

### 4. About Us & Google Maps Location
- Studio heritage, zero-waste metrics (18+ years, 0% templates, 94% waste diverted).
- Physical address in Mehrauli Heritage Corridor, New Delhi with interactive Google Maps embed and direct "Open in Google Maps" link.

### 5. Dual-Track Appointment Booking
- **Track 1: Client Commissions (Homeowners & Patrons)**
  - Open **Monday through Saturday** (Sundays are strictly closed and blocked in the date selector).
  - Collects client name, contact, project typology, site location, timeline, and brief.
- **Track 2: Business & Vendor Collaborations (Companies & Suppliers)**
  - Open **Strictly on Saturdays between 11:00 AM and 6:00 PM**.
  - The date picker validates that only a Saturday can be chosen.
  - The time slot dropdown only allows slots between 11:00 AM and 6:00 PM.
- Submissions generate a booking reference ID and are immediately saved to the Admin Panel.

### 6. Admin Portal (Studio CMS & Analytics)
- Access by clicking **"Studio Portal"** in the top navigation bar or **"Enter Admin Console"** in the footer.
- **Passcode:** `admin` (or `ruchir2026`).
- **Features in the Admin Panel:**
  1. **Traffic & Demographics Dashboard**: Real-time website visitor count, audience geographic breakdown (India, UAE, UK, US), device breakdown (Desktop, Mobile, Tablet).
  2. **Appointment Manager**: View client and vendor appointments, filter by category, confirm/set pending status, delete records, or **Export to CSV**.
  3. **Live Content CMS**: Edit website copy on the fly (Studio Title, Philosophy heading and narrative, About Us story, Contact info, phone, and Google Maps URL). Clicking **Publish CMS Updates** immediately updates the live front-end without reloading!
